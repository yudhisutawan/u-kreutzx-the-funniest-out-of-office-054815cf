# Tournament — The Funniest Out-of-Office

Competition: `u-kreutzx-the-funniest-out-of-office-054815cf` — one line,
max 140 characters. Judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash) picks the funnier of two
lines, weighing, in order:

1. A one-line out-of-office reply a real person would set and send.
2. Turns on a genuine surprise, a twist the reader does not see coming.
3. Sounds like a person with a voice of their own, not a template.
4. Stays fresh, avoiding jokes everyone has already read.
5. Uses every word; nothing could be cut without losing the laugh.

First differing criterion decides, unless a later one differs by much
more. Method per `win-gap-playbook.md`: 3+ candidates, pairwise
tournament, submit only the winner.
